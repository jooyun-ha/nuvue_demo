# Nuvue Vision Brief

Last updated: 2026-03-26

## 1. Vision

Nuvue helps people make better food decisions in the moment, with guidance that is personal, practical, and low-friction.

The long-term vision is an adaptive decision-support product that can meet users wherever they make food choices:

- inside a mobile app
- through camera-based assistance
- eventually through ambient or wearable experiences

## 2. Core Idea

Many people want to eat in a way that matches their goals, but real-world food decisions happen quickly and under pressure. Menus, portions, cravings, health goals, and incomplete information all collide in a short moment.

Nuvue is designed to reduce that decision burden. Instead of asking users to manually log every meal or memorize nutrition rules, the product helps them interpret what they are seeing and make a final choice with more confidence.

## 3. Problem

People often face food decisions when they have:

- limited time
- incomplete information
- multiple competing goals
- low mental energy

Existing solutions are often weak in at least one of these ways:

- too manual
- too educational but not actionable
- too generic
- too judgmental
- not available in the moment of choice

## 4. Product Promise

Nuvue gives users adaptive food guidance that feels:

- immediate
- understandable
- personalized
- supportive rather than restrictive

The app should help users think less, not feel worse.

## 5. Product Philosophy

Nuvue should avoid creating a feeling of "food prison" or health perfectionism. The product is not trying to control users. It is trying to support them with useful context and light decision assistance.

The user remains the final decision-maker.

## 6. MVP Thesis

If users can:

- set a lightweight profile
- capture food or menu context with a camera
- receive useful guidance quickly
- save what they decided

then we can validate whether real-time food decision support is a valuable product behavior.

## 7. MVP Focus

The first release should prove one strong loop:

1. user opens app
2. user chooses a food decision mode
3. user captures what they are seeing
4. app returns simple guidance
5. user saves their final decision

The initial modes are:

- Menu Deciding
- Portion Direction
- My History

## 8. Future Direction

If the MVP works, Nuvue can expand toward:

- stronger personalization
- richer recommendation logic
- pattern analysis over time
- specialist-assisted guidance
- wearable or smart glasses integration

The mobile app should be treated as the first proving ground, not the final form of the product.

## 9. What Success Looks Like

Success in the near term means:

- users understand the app quickly
- users trust the output enough to act on it
- users feel less friction when making food decisions
- users return to the app because the guidance is useful in context

## 10. Strategic Guardrails

- Start with a narrow, usable loop.
- Prioritize clarity over sophistication.
- Use personalization only where it improves relevance.
- Avoid medical claims beyond the team’s ability to support safely.
- Build trust before automation.
